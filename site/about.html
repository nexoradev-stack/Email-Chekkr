About Email Chekkr

Check every email before you send.

Why it exists

Every bounced email hurts your sender reputation a little, and enough of them push your campaigns into spam folders. Most bounces come from simple things: a typo like gmial.com, a domain that no longer exists, a temporary inbox someone used to grab a download, or a shared info@ address that nobody reads. Email Chekkr catches those before you hit send.

How it works

Every address goes through six checks: format, domain, mail server, temporary inbox, team address and typos. You get one of four results (valid, risky, invalid or unknown), a score from 0 to 100, a confidence level and a plain reason.

On this website the checks run inside your browser. Your CSV is never uploaded. To see whether a domain takes email, only the domain (for example example.com) is looked up, never the full address. The same checking engine also powers the API and the MCP server for apps and AI tools like Claude, Cursor and Gemini.

What we promise

  • Honest results. Email Chekkr doesn't log in to mail servers, so "valid" means the address and its domain look deliverable, not that the exact mailbox was confirmed. We say so everywhere.
  • Your list stays yours. No uploads from the website, no stored addresses in the API, no cookies, no analytics, no ad trackers.
  • Fair pricing. Single checks are always free. Paid bulk plans will be simple credit packs, not subscriptions you forget to cancel.
