Using Email Chekkr

You can use Email Chekkr to check email addresses that you have a lawful reason to hold, for example your own customers, subscribers or leads. Don't use it to build lists for spam or to break any law, including anti-spam and data protection laws.

What the results mean

Results are an estimate based on the address format and public information about its domain. Email Chekkr doesn't confirm that a specific mailbox exists. A "valid" result does not guarantee delivery, and an "invalid" or "risky" result may occasionally be wrong.
