      What each result means

      Every address gets one of four results and a score from 0 to 100.

      • Valid

        Well-formed, and the domain has a working mail server. No warning signs. Safe to send.

      • Risky

        The domain takes email, but something is off: a temporary inbox, a team address like info@, a likely typo, or no mail server record.

      • Invalid

        Badly formed, or the domain doesn't exist or doesn't accept email. Sending will bounce.

      • Unknown

        The domain didn't answer in time. Check it again later before removing it.

      What Truvio checks

      • The address is written correctly (one @, allowed characters, valid extension)
      • The domain exists
      • The domain has a mail server that accepts email
      • The domain isn't a temporary inbox service (10,000+ known domains)
      • The address isn't a team inbox like info@, sales@ or support@
      • Common typos, like gmial.com or company.con

      What it doesn't

      • Truvio doesn't log in to mail servers, so "valid" means the address and domain look deliverable — not that this exact mailbox was confirmed.
      • It never sends an email to anyone on your list.
      • It doesn't store your addresses. To check a domain, only the part after the @ (like acmecorp.com) is looked up.

      Questions

      Is my list uploaded anywhere?

      No. Your CSV is read and checked inside your browser. To see if a domain receives email, Truvio asks a public DNS service about the domain only (for example acmecorp.com) — never the full address.

      Why is an address "risky" and not "valid"?

      Its domain takes email, but there's a warning sign: it's a temporary inbox, a team address, a likely typo, or the domain has no mail server record. You decide whether to keep it.

      How many addresses can I check?

      Up to 10,000 rows per file. Bigger lists can be split into several files.
