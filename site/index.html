    Results

    What each result means

    Every address gets one of four results and a score from 0 to 100.

    • Valid

      Written correctly, and the domain has a working mail server. No warning signs. Safe to send.

    • Risky

      The domain takes email, but something is off: a temporary inbox, a team address like info@, a likely typo, or no mail server record.

    • Invalid

      Badly written, or the domain doesn't exist or doesn't accept email. Sending will bounce.

    • Unknown

      The domain didn't answer in time. Check it again later before removing it.

    Private by design

    Your list stays with you

    • Checks run in your browser. Your CSV is never uploaded.
    • Only the domain is looked up, for example example.com, never the full address.
    • No cookies, no analytics, no ad trackers.
    Good to know

    What it doesn't do

    • It doesn't log in to mail servers, so "valid" means the address and domain look deliverable, not that this exact mailbox was confirmed.
    • It never sends an email to anyone on your list.
    • It doesn't store your addresses anywhere.
    FAQ

    Questions

    Is my list uploaded anywhere?

    No. Your CSV is read and checked inside your browser. To see if a domain receives email, Email Chekkr asks a public DNS service about the domain only (for example example.com), never the full address.

    Why is an address "risky" and not "valid"?

    Its domain takes email, but there's a warning sign: a temporary inbox, a team address, a likely typo, or no mail server record. You decide whether to keep it.

    How many addresses can I check?

    Single checks are unlimited. A CSV can have up to 10,000 rows; bigger lists can be split into several files.

    Does it cost anything?

    Single checks are free. Bulk checks are free during early access, and paid plans from $5 are coming. See Pricing.

    Can I use it from my own app or an AI tool?

    Yes. There's a REST API and an MCP server for Claude, Cursor and other AI tools. See the API docs.
