Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

The short version

Truvio doesn't collect, store or share the email addresses you check. Checks run in your browser.

What happens when you check an address

  • The address and any CSV file you choose are read by your browser only. They are not uploaded to Truvio.
  • To see if a domain receives email, your browser asks a public DNS-over-HTTPS service about the domain only — for example acmecorp.com, never jane@acmecorp.com. Truvio uses Google Public DNS, with Cloudflare DNS as a backup. Their own privacy policies apply to those lookups.
  • Results stay in your browser until you close the page or download them.

Cookies and tracking

Truvio doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Fonts

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts download.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example when accounts or paid plans are added — the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact us.
